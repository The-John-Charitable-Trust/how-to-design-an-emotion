# Contributing

*How To Design an Emotion* is maintained as an author-governed, living scholarly work. The purpose of contribution is to improve the work while preserving authorship, provenance, intellectual coherence, and a reviewable history of change.

## Governance principle

**Everyone may read. Qualified contributors may propose. Authorized editors may approve.**

Public access to the repository does not grant permission to modify the canonical branch directly. Contributors should propose changes through the contribution workflow established by The John Charitable Trust.

## What may be proposed

Contributions may include corrections, source or citation improvements, methodological criticism, clarifications, figures, reproducibility improvements, or other changes that strengthen the scholarly work.

## Review

Proposed changes are reviewed before acceptance. Review may result in acceptance, requested revision, or rejection. The original author and authorized maintainers retain editorial authority over the canonical edition.

## Attribution and license

By submitting material for inclusion, a contributor represents that they have the right to submit it and agrees that accepted contributions may be distributed under the repository's CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.

## Manuscript integrity

The canonical manuscript lives in `book/`. Keep substantive changes narrowly scoped and explain the scholarly reason for each proposed change. Publication assets belong in `assets/`; archival source files belong in `source/`.
