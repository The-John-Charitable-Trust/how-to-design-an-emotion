# Authors and Contributors

## Original Author

**Cézjah**

Original author and creator of *How To Design an Emotion*.

The identity of the original author is permanent provenance and is not altered by subsequent contributions, forks, adaptations, or editions.

## Contributors

Accepted contributions will be preserved through Git history and, where appropriate, recognized here or in release notes. Contribution does not by itself confer original authorship, editorial authority, or ownership of the canonical work.

## Roles

- **Original Author** — creator of the originating work and its intellectual architecture.
- **Contributor** — a person whose proposed change has been accepted into the canonical repository.
- **Reviewer** — a person authorized to evaluate proposed changes.
- **Maintainer** — a person authorized to administer the repository and publication workflow.
